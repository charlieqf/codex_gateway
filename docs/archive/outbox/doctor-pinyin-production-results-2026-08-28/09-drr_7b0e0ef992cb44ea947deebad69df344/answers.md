## 问题 4

李为民医生近期有哪些已验证的发表论文？

**答案**: 经核实的发表文献涉及哮喘急性发作相关因素（久坐与体力活动、TyG指数）、肺癌肺栓塞预测模型（H\-X模型）及肺癌单细胞与空间转录组学综述等方向；具体篇目由系统核实添加。

**已验证来源**: [Asthmatics with increased triglyceride\-glucose index are at elevated risk of exacerbations independent of type 2 inflammation: A prospective cohort study\.](<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42600980/>) (src\_pubmed\_42600980); [H\-X model: A novel tool for predicting pulmonary embolism in lung cancer\.](<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42624780/>) (src\_pubmed\_42624780); [Deciphering lung cancer at high resolution: a narrative review of applications of single\-cell and spatial transcriptomics sequencing\.](<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42582974/>) (src\_pubmed\_42582974)
